# Syllabus

Mission: connect statistical foundations to predictive-modeling judgment and practical Python.

1. Sampling distributions, LLN/CLT, dependence and bootstrap (current).
2. Estimation, bias, variance, consistency, likelihood and maximum likelihood.
3. Confidence intervals, tests, power, effect sizes and multiple comparisons.
4. Linear regression, least squares, conditional expectation and diagnostics.
5. Generalization, validation, regularization and data leakage.
6. Classification, logistic regression, loss, metrics and calibration.
7. Flexible models: splines, trees, forests and boosting.
8. Bayesian modeling and posterior prediction.
9. Experimental design, A/B tests, confounding and causal vs predictive questions.
10. Applied end-to-end case, time/group splits, distribution shift and interview defense.

Review probability/distributions when necessary. Use frequent retrieval, guided exercises, derivations and Python.
